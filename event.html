Event management is the systematic application of project management principles to the creation, organization, and execution of public and private gatherings. Whether applied to intimate personal celebrations like weddings or large-scale corporate conferences, festivals, and trade shows, the discipline bridges creative vision with operational logistics. It requires coordinating multi-faceted elements—including budgeting, venue selection, vendor management, scheduling, and risk mitigation—to deliver a seamless, goal-driven experience that meets stakeholder objectives and engages the target audience.
